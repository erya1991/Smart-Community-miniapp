## Brand & Style

This design system reflects the lived history and contemporary renewal of Nanjing's historic Daguang Road in the Qinhuai District. It rejects both the sterile, alienating rigidity of administrative government blues and the superficial, costumed palette of tourist-kitsch historical styles. Instead, it captures the everyday cadence of Jiangnan alleyways: weather-patinated brickwork, ancient pagoda tiles, whitewashed plaster walls softening with age, and the warm domestic glow of evening streetlights along the riverbank.

The design movement combines **Contemporary Restrained Humanism** with **Modernized Jiangnan Regionalism**. Whitespace is generous, soft, and warm. Structures are stable and legibly rhythmic, tailored with high contrast and natural legibility for multi-generational civic life (including senior-friendly accessibility). The interface balances quiet scholarly dignity with the warmth of neighborhood mutual aid, clean civic renewal, and modern community digital services.

## Colors

The palette grounds the interface in authentic architectural and topographical pigments of Nanjing’s southern old city:

- **Primary (`#1B4D53` - Qin-Huai Ink Teal / 黛青):** Sourced from the intersection of wet Ming city wall masonry and the quiet, deep waters of the Qinhuai River at dusk. Authoritative, dignified, and visually soothing without corporate chill.
- **Secondary (`#C85A32` - City Wall Kiln Terracotta / 砖赭):** Derived from historical low-fired roof tiles and street-level clay masonry. Serves as the energetic action anchor for key calls to action, active statuses, subsidies, and community notifications.
- **Tertiary (`#2E7256` - Canal Willow & Fresh Produce Green / 溪柳绿):** A grounded herbal green representing organic life, community garden initiatives, fresh neighborhood markets, and primary municipal convenience functions.
- **Neutral (`#26292B` - Smoked Ink Stone / 徽墨黑):** A warm charcoal replacing pure digital black, providing crisp, fatigue-free reading contrast across typographic hierarchies.

### Surface Tones & Canvases
- **Background Base (`#FAF8F5` - Plaster White / 暖墙白):** An eye-friendly mineral warm off-white, echoing lime plaster under natural daylight. Eliminates harsh screen glare for elderly residents.
- **Surface Layer 1 (`#F3EFEA` - River Pebble Greige / 浅米灰):** Subtle containment background for grouped cards, utility modules, and inputs.
- **Surface Layer 2 (`#E9E3DA` - Weathered Sandstone / 夯土米色):** Delimiting strokes, structural division lines, and subtle inactive pills.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy couples **sourceSerif4** for editorial titles and landmark headings with **publicSans** for interfaces, navigation, and reading text.

- **Headlines (`sourceSerif4`):** Possesses literary weight, classical proportion, and clear humanist stroke terminals, honoring the scholarly past of southern Nanjing without ornate sentimentality.
- **Interface & Reading (`publicSans`):** An exceptionally legible, balanced neo-grotesque optimized for public service applications. Its generous x-height and distinct glyph apertures ensure complete legibility for older citizens or low-glare outdoor viewing.
- **Senior Accessibility Adjustment:** Base body copy never defaults below `16px` in core reading flows or municipal service tasks. Key metadata labels preserve strict letterform spacing and high contrast against `#FAF8F5`.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored on an 8pt base grid system, providing a stable, calm cadence:

- **Mobile Viewports (<640px):** Single-column stack with `16px` edge margins. Primary touch targets maintain an explicit `48px` minimum height to preserve physical ergonomics for older users.
- **Tablet & Medium Displays (640px–1024px):** 8-column layout with `20px` gutters and `24px` lateral margins. Secondary data shifts into persistent side panels.
- **Desktop Screens (>1024px):** 12-column grid capped at a maximum content width of `1200px` to maintain comfortable eye movement.
- **Rhythmic Densities:** Generous spatial buffers are placed between administrative blocks and service groups to avoid perceptual clutter and cognitive fatigue.

## Elevation & Depth

Spatial hierarchy relies on tactile tonal layering and atmospheric warmth rather than stark artificial drop shadows:

- **Level 0 (Canvas):** `#FAF8F5` flat ground plane.
- **Level 1 (Structural Containers & Cards):** `#FFFFFF` with a quiet, low-contrast boundary line (`1px solid #EAE4DA`) paired with an ambient tinted shadow: `0 2px 8px -1px rgba(27, 77, 83, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Popovers, Active Drops, Bottom Sheets):** `#FFFFFF` surface elevated by a deeper atmospheric shadow infused with ink teal: `0 10px 24px -4px rgba(27, 77, 83, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(38, 41, 43, 0.04)`.
- **Dividers & Delimiters:** Always execute horizontal structural separators using `#EAE4DA` at `1px` width; avoid solid gray or pure black vectors.

## Shapes

The design uses **Rounded (`2`)** geometry, mapping base corners to `0.5rem (8px)`, compound container corners to `1rem (16px)`, and large structural dialogs or bottom sheets to `1.5rem (24px)`.

This curvature replicates the naturally softened edges of weathered Ming stone masonry and rounded riverside river-walk timber, striking an intentional balance: neither aggressively blocky (brutalist) nor toy-like and overly circular (pill/bubble). Form fields, cards, and modal components remain clearly architectural and solid.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background in Qin-Huai Ink Teal (`#1B4D53`), text in `#FFFFFF`, corner radius `8px`. Active state transitions subtly to `#143B40`. Minimum touch height: `48px`.
- **Secondary / Action Alert:** Background in City Wall Terracotta (`#C85A32`), text in `#FFFFFF`. Reserved for high-urgency notifications, immediate booking confirmations, and special community initiatives.
- **Outline / Quiet:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #1B4D53`, text `#1B4D53`. Never uses thin `1px` low-contrast lines on interactive triggers.

### Cards
- Standard containers use solid `#FFFFFF` over `#FAF8F5`, bounded by a `1px` border of `#EAE4DA` with an `8px` corner radius.
- Padding sits comfortably at `16px` for dense data or `20px` for civic reading articles. 
- Avoid heavy saturated card headers; use a calm secondary title with an inline terracotta indicator pip (`6px` circle in `#C85A32`) for active context.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- **Default:** Background `#F3EFEA`, border `1px solid #E4DDD2`, text `#26292B`.
- **Selected:** Background `#1B4D53`, border `1px solid #1B4D53`, text `#FFFFFF`.
- Sizing adheres to a minimum height of `36px` to guarantee confident tap accuracy.

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- Surface is `#FFFFFF` with a `1.5px solid #DDD6CB` border. Focus state shifts border color to `#1B4D53` accompanied by a soft `3px` focus ring tinted to `rgba(27, 77, 83, 0.15)`.
- Labels are persistently visible in `label-md` weight above fields, avoiding floating placeholders that degrade usability for senior demographics.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size set to `22px × 22px` for ease of touch.
- Selected state adopts `#1B4D53` with crisp white check marks (`2.5px` stroke width).

### Localized Service Components
- **Notice Bar (Community Announcements):** Light ochre-tinted warm background (`#F9F4EB`), with an ink-teal left structural anchor accent bar (`4px` width, `#1B4D53`).
- **Senior Quick Access Panels:** Generous dual-action blocks (`64px` height) incorporating high-legibility icons with plain-language descriptive labels in `#26292B`.
